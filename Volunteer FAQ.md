# Volunteer FAQ

This page will help you find some answers to your questions about CodeYourFuture (CYF) and our courses. 

## I am interested in volunteering

- **Am I eligible to volunteer?**
    
    Our eligibility criteria are:
    
    - You must be over 18 years old.
    - You must be able to volunteer consistently for at least 6 months, volunteering 4 hours per week. *(We have flexibility for holidays, etc)*
    - You must spend at least 20 minutes weekly catching up and messaging with volunteers and trainees at CYF on Slack
    - You must adhere to our Code of Conduct and the Volunteer agreement
    - You must adhere to our values and working principles
- **How can I help as a volunteer?**
    
    We have plenty of roles depending on your experience and on the skills you want to improve. Think about what you want out of this experience at CodeYourFuture - other than the standard giving back, helping society and making the world more diverse.
    
    The questions we’ll ask you to reflect on are:
    
    1. Do you want to volunteer in person or remotely?
    2. How many hours a week can you commit (minimum 4 hours)?
    3. Do you want to volunteer during the week or on Saturdays?
    4. What is your professional experience? (under 2 years, 2-5 years, over 5 years)
    
    These will help us identify the best role for you. See below some examples of the support you can give us:
    
    **No professional experience**
    
    - Class Facilitator
        - Lead a Basic Online Skills class, which happens on weekdays
        - Lead an Intro to Digital workshop on Saturdays
        - Lead  Intro to Digital online workshops on Wednesdays evening
    - Coursework reviewer
        - Review Basic Online Skills submissions
        - Review Intro to Digital exercises
    
    **Professional with 2-5 years of experience**
    
    - Class Facilitator
        - Lead an Intro to Programming course on Saturdays
    - Career mentor: support one or multiple trainees (flexible and online)
    - Employability reviewer: you can review CVs, do mock interviews, and/or give demo feedback to help our trainees’ employability skills.
    - Pair Programmer: work on a 1-2-1 basis with a trainee to enhance their technical skills
    - Code reviewer: review 10 pull requests a week to ensure our trainees get valuable feedback on their work
    
    **More than 5 years of experience**
    
    - Class Facilitator
        - Lead in-person Saturday classes in your local Software Development Course.
        - Lead remote Saturday classes for regions that aren’t running locally
    - Code Reviewer: review 10 pull requests a week to ensure our trainees get valuable feedback on their work
    
    **Other ways of helping**
    
    - You can project manage our community events
    - You can work with us on our marketing strategy
    - You can represent us at events to do outreach for volunteers, trainees or partnerships, as well as speaking at events

- **I can only volunteer for a few hours. What’s the minimum expected commitment?**
    
    We have some roles that have a reduced number of hours and/or just happen every other month.
    
    We expect you to volunteer at least 4 hours a week.
    
- **I can only volunteer remotely.**
    
    We have some roles that can be done remotely, for example, code reviewer and career mentor. It will depend on your capacity to support us.
    
- **I don’t have professional experience. Can I still volunteer?**
    
    Yes, you can support Intro to Digital and Basic Online Skills courses.
    
- **I have no technical background. Can I still volunteer?**
    
    Yes! We need people from a wide range of backgrounds to support trainees with the other professional skills they must have to get their jobs in tech.
    
- **I want to volunteer in person.**
    
    Our classes run on Saturdays from 10-5, so we’d expect you to be there at 9.45. Sign up on the Class Planner, so we all know you are joining us.
    
- **What teaching method do you use?**
    
    The CYF curriculum is based on the flipped classroom model. We expect trainees to start building their understanding independently as soon as possible. From the beginning of the course, trainees are expected to prepare and take ownership of their learning before they attend in-person sessions. 
    
    This video can help you understand the flipped classroom.
    
    https://youtu.be/r2b7GeuqkPc
    
    The classes aren’t lectures. At CYF we have to *maximise the time we have together* by working in person on projects, hitting blockers, and learning together. 
    
    We are not a school. We are a community of professionals coming together to share skills and build real things to help people get real jobs within one year.
    
    Time is very important at CYF. We try our best to use everyone’s time as well as we can. We want to use our time together to help learners with the things they can’t do at home: debugging, pair programming, code review, and building projects.

## I signed up - next step

- **I signed up. What happens now?**
    
    You should have received an email with a link to book yourself into the next available Welcome Session. *Check your junk/spam folder too, please.*
    
    If you have not received this email, please contact us!
    
- **Tell me about the onboarding process**
    
    These are steps for onboarding:
    
    1. Sign-up
    2. Receive sign-up confirmation email with link for first session
    3. Welcome Session: find out more about CYF
    4. Receive an email with a presentation and quiz, sign the Volunteer agreement, read about our Code of Conduct and Safeguarding policies
    5. Receive an invite to our Slack workspace
    6. Introduce yourself on the channel #cyf-volunteers
    7. Do the relevant trainings (Edu Training, Tools Training, Code review training, etc)
    8. Come to the nearest class to you to get to know people
    9. Watch our Slack Essentials at CYF video
    10. Start contributing
- **How do I find my community, volunteers or trainees in my region?**
    
    That’s a great question! The community is the heart of CodeYourFuture, so knowing how to get in touch with them is crucial!
    
    The main online place to get in touch with your community is on Slack, a platform for collaboration and instant communication.
    
    You will get access to Slack once you have done the onboarding process.
    
    On Slack, we have community channels, in which all trainees and volunteers are in. And we have volunteer channels, in which trainees are not in. Join the relevant channels for you

- **I arrived at the class. What do I do?**
    
    There should be other volunteers in class - so identify them and introduce yourself, explaining this is your first time here.
    
    Talk to anyone else who is in class, always clearly stating it’s your first day with us.
    
- **What happens when the course finishes?**
    
    When the course finishes, the next one starts. Our calendar runs all year long, so you can join the next group of volunteers, since you’ll be very helpful in supporting new volunteers now. 
    
- **What is Slack and how does it work?**
    
    Slack is the operating system that brings people together. And because we all just meet on Saturdays, we need a way of talking to each other during the week too. Slack is that place. 
    
    Slack is also a software used actively in the tech industry for communication. So by learning how to use it, you’ll already know how to get around it on your first job.
    
    Our Slack is organised as follows:
    
    - Intro to Digital and Basic Online Skills workspace
    - CYF Community workspace: includes all trainees and volunteers
    
    All workspaces have the same channel structure, so search for keywords such as your region i.e. “London” and find the channels that you’d like to join, for example, #london-community
    
    Our automated workflows will invite you to the most important channels, but some channels might be missing, so join them yourself.
    
- **I can't find/access the Slack workspace. What do I do?**
    
    If you haven't received an invite, check the email address you applied with. The invites are sent after the volunteer agreement has been signed, and the quiz has been completed. If it's been more than 5 working days and you still don't have access, email volunteering@codeyourfuture.io with your full name and the email you applied with.
    
- **Where do I find the fire safety information of the venue?**
    
    This information will be available on our Slack channel as well as at the venue itself.
    
    Please go to your community channel (i.e. #scotland-community) and check the pinned documents on the top of the channel
    
- **Where do I send my feedback to?**
    
    The answer depends on what type of feedback.
    
    If it's something related to you to make your community better, share it on Slack or during a class with the community. Sharing what is going well or isn’t going well is very important to make your day-to-day better.
    
    If your feedback is something direct to one person (trainee, volunteers, staff), we suggest you talk to them directly. We suggest using the SBI feedback model (Situation, Behaviour, Impact) to do so.
    
- **Who can help me with a referral or reference letter?**
    
    Once you have completed 12 months of volunteering at least 4 hours a week at CodeYourFuture, we’ll happily provide you with a reference letter (for example, for a job application, a visa, or a course).
    
    These letters use a template in which we state which region you are in, what type of volunteering you supported us with, and explain CYF and the impact.
    
    To request this, please fill in this form:
    
    👉 Request a Reference or Letter of Support
    
    Tell us what you need and why. This helps us write the right letter for you.
    
    Please allow enough time before your deadline. We will take up to 5 working days to reply to you.
    
- **Does CodeYourFuture help volunteers get jobs too?**
    
    No. Our primary focus and resources are dedicated to supporting our trainees into software development and tech careers.
    However, we are happy to support volunteers with **employment referrals** once they have been consistently volunteering with CodeYourFuture for **over 12 months.**
    
- **I need help finding information about a problem or a question**
    
    If in doubt, reach out. We are here to support you. 
    
    We might not always be able to solve your problem, but we can at least help you find a way when we have contacts.

## Supporting trainees

- **How should we advise students about their use of AI whilst learning at CYF?**
    
    As volunteers, you have a huge range of experience with AI as well as opinions about its role in learning. So we've designed some guidelines that can help you guide students in the right direction to learn, ask the right questions around AI use, and prioritise learning objectives as the courses progress. You can find a quick cheat sheet here or the full guidelines here
    
- **How can I help with job searches?**
    
    If you come across a good opportunity, post it on the #cyf-employment.
    
    We also have a Wednesday online session to work together on this. We believe that searching together helps our trainees to build up their resilience in this difficult process. Read more on this guide: https://curriculum.codeyourfuture.io/guides/employability/job-search/collective-exercise/
    
- **How do I help with employability, interview prep, CV review?**
    
    Join the relevant channels, introduce yourself and get notifications of trainees asking for help:
    
    - #cyf-interview-practice
    - #cyf-profile-review
    - #cyf-employment
- **How do I know if my trainees are doing well or falling behind?**
    
    You can request access to our Dashboard system, and you can see how many steps they have submitted - and if this is in line with the expectation, considering where you are at on the course.
    
    Search for the questions about system access to find more information.
    
- **Where can I see how many trainees we have in the course?**
    
    You can request access to our Dashboard system, and you can filter your region and cohort, so you know how many trainees should be coming to class.
    
    Search for the questions about system access to find more information.
    
- **How do I review coursework and pull requests?**
    
    Follow these steps to review Intro to Programming and Software Development Course submissions:
    
    1. Join the Slack channel #cyf-code-review-volunteer-team
    2. Read our **Code Review Guide** to understand how we review code at CYF
    3. Learn about the **Code Reviewer role** in this document.
    4. Attend a Code Review **training session**
    5. **Join the CYF GitHub Organisation**: fill in the Reviewer Application Form
    6. Check out the **Code Review Resources**
    7. Start reviewing:
        1. Intro to Programming PRs Needing Review
        2. Software Development Course PRs Needing Review 
- **How do I start reviewing the Intro to Digital steps?**
    
    You must request access to our Dashboard system, and you can see how many steps they have submitted - and if this is in line with the expectation, considering where you are in the course.
    
    Important resources:
    
    - Join the Slack channel: #cyf-intro-to-digital-course-volunteers
    - The acceptance criteria for the coursework are on this page: https://curriculum.codeyourfuture.io/itd/
    
    Search for the questions about system access to find more information.
    
- **How do I start pair programming?**
    
    Follow these steps to pair programming with Intro to Programming or Software Development Course trainees:
    
    1. Join the Slack channel: #cyf-pair-programming
    2. Read this guidance on how we pair programme at CodeYourFuture
    3. Use a calendar tool to tell people what your availability is. ****Example: Calendly, Doodle, Google Calendar
    4. Share your availability on the canvas bookmarked on the Slack channel.
- **How do I start mentoring someone?**
    
    Follow these steps to become a career mentor:
    
    1. Join the Slack channel: #cyf-career-mentors
    2. Read our https://curriculum.codeyourfuture.io/guides/employability/ to understand how we work with employability at CodeYourFuture
    3. Join our onboarding sessions - sign up using this link
    4. Use a calendar tool to tell people what your availability is. ****Example: Calendly, Doodle, Google Calendar
    5. Add your name, calendar link and LinkedIn link to the ****Career Mentor Directory spreadsheet
    6. One trainee will select you and book a time in with you

## I would like to leave

- **I want a new role**
    1. Let the current team you are supporting know you no longer want to do this specific role. Make sure you hand over any tasks and find a replacement for yourself, so your community isn’t let down
    2. If you know what you want to do, join that specific channel. If you are not sure yet, reach out to a more experienced volunteer or a staff member, and have a chat about the options you have
- **I can’t or don’t want to support CYF anymore**
    
    We understand this can happen. 
    
    Let the current team you are supporting know you no longer want to do this specific role. Make sure you hand over any tasks and find a replacement for yourself, so your community isn’t let down.
    
    If you want all your data to be deleted, please email us at ops AT codeyourfuture.io (replace AT by @ and remove the spaces.
